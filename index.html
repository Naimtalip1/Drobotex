01

MISSION BRIEF

Two days where Sarawak's brightest minds pit drones, robots and code against each other — on the floor, in the air, and in the arena.

DROBOTEX 2026 is TEGAS's flagship robotics and drone exposition — a convergence of competition, exhibition and education. Nine competition arenas run across two categories: Category A for schools and Category B for open and tertiary teams, backed by universities, TVET institutions and industry builders from across Borneo.
