EVENT RECAP & MISSION BRIEF

Two days where Sarawak's brightest minds pitted drones, robots and code against each other — on the floor, in the air, and in the arena.

DROBOTEX 2026 has concluded with remarkable success as TEGAS's flagship robotics and drone exposition — a massive convergence of competition, exhibition, and hands-on education. Nine competition arenas welcomed schools, tertiary students, and open teams backed by universities, TVET institutions and industry builders from across Borneo.
