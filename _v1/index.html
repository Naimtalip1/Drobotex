// 01 — ABOUT THE PROGRAM

A two-day mission control for young innovators

Drone and Robotics Explore (DROBOTEX) 2026 is a two-day event combining competitions, exhibitions, and workshops on drones and robotics. Built for school students and youth, it's designed to ignite passion for innovation, technology, and engineering.

Participants will engage in exciting competitions, hands-on activities, and workshops — showcasing the real-world potential of drones and robotics in future industries.
